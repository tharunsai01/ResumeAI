# User guide

## Candidates

1. **Create an account.** Choose "I'm looking for work". Your profile starts empty.
2. **Upload a resume** in Resume hub. Drag in a PDF or DOCX (up to 5 MB). You can watch each stage: uploading, validating, extracting text, detecting skills, analysing experience, generating the report.
3. **Review the analysis.** Check the skills, roles and education that were found, then choose **Copy to profile**. Skills you don't already list are added; experience, education and summary are filled only where your profile is empty. Edit anything that's wrong on the Profile page.
4. **Discover jobs.** Search by title, company or skill, and filter by work mode, employment type, location, salary and experience. Bookmark jobs to find them later under Saved jobs.
5. **Read your match.** Open a job and choose **See my match**. You get a score out of 100 made of four parts (skills 50%, experience 20%, education 10%, role fit 20%), with matched, related and missing skills, strengths, gaps and what would raise the score.
6. **Apply** with an optional cover letter. Your active resume is shared with that recruiter only.
7. **Track applications.** Each application shows a status tracker and a history of every change. Interview invitations show the time and a meeting link. You can withdraw an application that is still open.
8. **Skill analysis** lists the skills most often requested by the jobs you applied to or saved, and which skills you already have that bridge the gap.

Turn on **Visible in the talent pool** on your profile if you want recruiters to find you before you apply.

## Recruiters

1. **Create an account** as "I'm hiring" with your company name, then complete your Company profile.
2. **Post a job.** Paste the description and choose **Suggest skills from description** to fill required and preferred skills, experience and education. Review the suggestions, then save a draft or publish. The preview shows the job as candidates see it.
3. **Screen applicants.** AI screening ranks applicants by the official formula and marks them as screened. It never shortlists or rejects anyone. Open a candidate to see the full breakdown, their cover letter and resume, and add private notes. Select two or three applicants to compare them side by side.
4. **Decide.** Shortlist or mark as not a fit, with an optional reason. Candidates are notified.
5. **Run the pipeline.** Drag cards between Applied, Screened, Shortlisted, Interview, Hired and Rejected, or use a card's menu. Moves that skip required steps are refused. Hiring and rejecting ask for confirmation.
6. **Schedule interviews** from the Interviews page (week view or list). Clashes with your other interviews are refused. Record feedback with a 1 to 5 rating and a recommendation.

Screening settings per job: automatic scoring on apply, blind review (hides names in the ranked list) and the highlight threshold. Scoring weights are fixed.

## Administrators

- **Overview**: live counts, 30-day activity and alerts for flagged resumes and open tickets.
- **Users**: search, filter, view history, suspend (a reason is required and the user is signed out everywhere) and reactivate.
- **Support tickets**: reply, and change status and priority. Changes are kept as resolution history.
- **Skill taxonomy**: add or edit skills, aliases and related skills; review unknown skills found in resumes.
- **Security audit**: flagged resumes with the exact findings. Clear or reject each one. Also lists failed sign-ins, lockouts and token reuse.
- **AI evaluation**: run the synthetic fairness benchmark or evaluate a real job's applicants. Reports cover counterfactual fairness, stability, the four-fifths rule, formula validation, score distribution and a model comparison. Runs are stored for comparison.
- **Audit log**: every security-relevant action, filterable by category, severity and text.
- **System health**: API, database and AI service status, latency, error rate and uptime, refreshed every 15 seconds.

## Everyone

- **Theme**: light, dark or match system, from the header or Settings. Signed-in users have it saved to their account.
- **Notifications**: the bell shows unread updates; the full list is under Notifications.
- **Help and support**: open a ticket and follow replies.
- **Settings**: change your password (other devices are signed out) and notification preferences.
